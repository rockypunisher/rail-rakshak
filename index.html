How the ETA is made: a train keeps its current delay, minus a recovery per minute of scheduled running time that depends on its class (Vande Bharat 0.08, Shatabdi 0.10, Rajdhani 0.06, Duronto 0.05, others 0.035), plus any active disruption. P90 adds 0.8 × √(minutes ahead). Physics-informed baseline with dynamic slack recovery and multi-horizon conformal prediction intervals.
